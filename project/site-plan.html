Website Planning Document

Heavenly Moments

Event Planning, Décor, Catering & Picnic Experiences

Site Name Purpose Scenarios Color Schema Typography Wireframes

1. Site Name

Heavenly Moments

The name "Heavenly Moments" communicates the business's aim to create beautiful, memorable experiences for its clients. It suits a company that plans events, decorates venues, provides catering, and creates picnic experiences.

Proposed domain: heavenlymoments.co.zw (availability has not been checked).

2. Site Purpose

The website will provide a professional online presence for Heavenly Moments, an events business based in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. It will introduce the business and its story, explain its event planning, décor, picnic and catering services, present Bronze, Silver and Gold picnic packages, and display a gallery of setups and food presentations.

Visitors will be able to find contact details, follow the business on social media, and submit an enquiry or booking request through an online form.

3. Visitor Scenarios

These questions represent information potential customers may look for when visiting the website.

  • What picnic packages are available, and what is included in the Bronze, Silver and Gold options?
  • Can Heavenly Moments provide décor and catering for a birthday, wedding or other special event in Bulawayo?
  • How can I view examples of previous event setups and food presentations?
  • How do I contact Heavenly Moments or submit a booking enquiry?

4. Color Schema

The palette combines a rich plum color with soft blush pink and light neutral backgrounds to create an elegant, welcoming event-planning brand.

Deep Plum — #6D3B63

Used for the main header, section headings, navigation and footer backgrounds, and primary buttons.

Blush Pink — #E9C6D8

Used for accents, highlighted areas, decorative borders, and hover states.

Soft Cream — #FFF9FC

Used as the main page background to keep the design light and readable.

Charcoal — #333333

Used for body text to provide strong contrast against light backgrounds.

5. Typography

Georgia — Headings

Georgia will be used for the site title and section headings to create an elegant, formal feel.

Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif — Body Text

This sans-serif font stack will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, forms and buttons because it is clear and easy to read on desktop and mobile screens.
